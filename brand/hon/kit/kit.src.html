The pack, front and back

Vector files: brand/hon/pack/hon-pouch-front.svg and hon-pouch-back.svg (1 unit = 1 mm, opens in Illustrator). Every number comes from the DATA block in build_pack.py, so edit there and re-run.

All four share the same DATA, legal content and layout grid, so they can be A/B tested with identical claims. D6's rooftop art is a vector stand-in; commission or generate the final illustration (IMG and LOGO-2 prompts) and drop it into the same zone.

Front, top to bottom

  1. Veg mark, 12.6 mm square on a white tile. This panel is 548 cm², and FSSAI requires at least 12 mm square above 500 cm².
  2. HON wordmark: "HON" in Bagel Fat One, outlined, never retyped or redrawn. Its colour follows the pouch colourway.
  3. HEALTH SUPPLEMENT in bold caps right under the brand name. The Nutra Regs 2022 require this on the front.
  4. Product name + descriptor. Names the forms, so no one has to guess.
  5. The yellow number. The dose is the hero, on every SKU, always in HON Butter.
  6. Three claim chips. 0g added sugar, melatonin-free, individually wrapped. Only claims the formula and pack can prove.
  7. Net quantity: 360 g (60 N × 6 g), on the front panel as Legal Metrology requires, with the 30-day supply and the "adults 18+" target group.
  8. Front text size: every line is set at ≥ 4.2 mm font size, about 3 mm cap height. FSS (L&D) Table I requires 3 mm letters on a front panel of 500–2,500 cm².

Back: everything the law and the buyer ask for

  1. QR to this batch's lab report at the top, not buried. Man Matters and Be Bodywise buyers complain about exactly this trust gap.
  2. Nutrition table per serve and per 100 g, with %RDA. Magnesium is measured against ICMR-NIN 2020 (440 mg men / 370 mg women).
  3. Magnesium by form, drawn to scale: the dose split as a bar.
  4. Ingredients in descending order, with INS numbers and the added-flavour declaration.
  5. Mandatory statements: NOT FOR MEDICINAL USE, recommended usage, "not to exceed…", "not a substitute for a varied diet", "out of reach of children", contraindications, and the not-intended-to-diagnose line.
  6. Boxed sweetener declaration: "CONTAINS NON-CALORIC SWEETENER" plus the stevia/monk fruit admixture line, required by Schedule II, with letters ≥ 3 mm measured on the letter l.
  7. Legal block: FSSAI logo and licence numbers, marketer and manufacturer, customer care, MRP incl. taxes, unit sale price (₹14.98/N), batch/MFG/best-before inkjet window, EAN-13.
  8. Back text size: 2.95 mm font (≈ 2.1 mm cap). Table I is written for the front panel; this matches what Be Bodywise ships on the same format. Have your label consultant confirm.

Logo

The HON logo is "HON" in Bagel Fat One, outlined, in 12 approved colourways (below). The lettering never changes; only the ground and letter colours do. The prompts further down are earlier explorations, kept for reference.

  1. Generate

    Firefly → Generate → Text to vector (Image tab). Model: Firefly (Adobe). Content type: Subject. Effect: Minimalism. Optional style reference: hon-logo-cherry.svg exported as PNG.

    Firefly web

  2. Open in Illustrator

    Use "Open in Illustrator" or download the SVG. Keep the shapes and ideas; throw away the generated letters.

    Illustrator

  3. Rebuild and own it

    Redraw with the Ellipse, Rectangle and Shape Builder tools, and set any words in a licensed font (Obviously Extended Black or Archivo Expanded Black). Test at 16 px (favicon) and 8 mm (wrapper). Then run an IP India image search and file in Class 5.

    Illustrator · IP India
Why rebuild instead of shipping the AI file?

The US Copyright Office's Jan 2025 report says prompts alone don't make you the author; human selection and modification do. Indian law is still unsettled, and AI marks can look like existing marks. A hand-rebuilt logo is cleaner to register and defend.

Pouch directions: 10 looks

Each direction has a concept render (the pouch in a styled scene) and a flat front design (for your designer). Generate 4 variations of each and judge the direction, not the lettering: Firefly will mangle small text, so the prompts keep only "HON" and "250mg" readable. Compliance travels with any direction: the veg mark stays visible, HEALTH SUPPLEMENT sits by the brand name, and legal text needs a light panel on dark or kraft looks.

How to choose fast

Shortlist 3. Put each concept render into the same Instagram-grid mockup and a Blinkit-style white tile, and show them to 10 people in your target group for 3 seconds each: "which would you pick up, and what do you think it does?" Chosen: D1 Bold Colour-Block, D5 Honest Type, D6 Indian Night Story, plus D10 Split Band (Be Bodywise-style structure). All four are built to scale in the Pack section (use the tabs) and share the same legal content, ready for an A/B test. D9 can still layer onto any of them.

Why not just prompt the pouch?

The studio pipeline

Image models garble small text. No prompt will reproduce your back panel, and the front's claims can drift. So the real artwork goes into every shot, and AI builds the world around it.

  1. Flats → 3D pouch

    Place the SVG flats on a stand-up-pouch mockup (a smart object) and export front, 3/4 and back angles as PNG with transparency.

    Illustrator → Photoshop

  2. Generate the set

    Use the prompts below with a plain stand-in pouch (Mode A), or edit a shot around your mockup render (Mode B).

    Firefly · Image Model 5

  3. Composite + Harmonize

    Place the real pouch PNG over the stand-in. Harmonize matches light, colour and shadow. It works on pixel layers only, so rasterise the smart object first.

    Photoshop · Harmonize

  4. Fix and upscale

    Use AI Markup or Generative Fill for local fixes, then Generative Upscale for 4K PDP and print.

    Photoshop · Firefly Edit

  5. Animate stills

    Use image-to-video with your composite as the first frame (and a last frame for transitions), so the label stays yours.

    Firefly · Video

  6. Edit, type, sound

    Cut in Premiere and hold end cards with Generative Extend. Set all on-screen text yourself, never generated.

    Premiere · Firefly Soundtrack
Commercial safety.

Firefly's own models (Image 5, Firefly Video) are the ones Adobe positions as commercially safe. The partner models inside Firefly (Nano Banana 2, Veo 3.1, Runway Gen-4.5, Kling) are great for exploring, but render final ad assets on Adobe's models or re-shoot the winning idea there.

Lock the look with a Custom Model (public beta).

Once you have 10–30 approved HON composites that look alike (≥1024 px, same lighting language), train a Firefly Custom Model on them as a "photographic style." Adobe says custom models are optimised for ideation, so still composite the real pack for finals.

  • Hands have five fingers and a natural grip; no one covers the logo
  • Shadows fall one way per scene; the contact shadow is present
  • No other brands readable in props (bathroom and travel shots)
  • Final ad assets rendered on Adobe's Firefly models
  • Ads carry "Health supplement. Not for medicinal use."
