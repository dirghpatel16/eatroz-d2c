# HON — High On Nutrition

Brand and pack source for HON Magnesium Sleep Gummies (India, FSSAI).

- `pack/build_pack.py`: single source of truth. Every number printed on the pouch lives in its `DATA` block, and each direction's colours live in `THEMES`. Run `python3 pack/build_pack.py` to regenerate everything below.
- `pack/hon-{d1,d5,d6,d10}-{front,back}.svg`: to-scale flats for the layout directions (D1 Bold Colour-Block, D5 Honest Type, D6 Indian Night Story, D10 Split Band with a Be Bodywise-style structure). They are 215 × 255 mm with an 80 mm gusset (1 SVG unit = 1 mm) and open in Illustrator. The `*-dieline` and `*-draft-flags` groups are guide layers; hide them before print.
- `pack/flats/hon-ag1-<colourway>-flat.svg`: printer flats: front + back side by side, 80 mm gusset (40 mm per side), 3 mm bleed, crop marks, dieline layer.
- `pack/hon-ag1-<colourway>-{front,back}.svg`: AG1-style pouches (flood, oversized logo, three-number spec strip) with logo v2, in 10 colourways; see `AG1_INFO`.
- `pack/hon-p{1..10}-{front,back}.svg`: 10 packaging designs (P1–P5 US-inspired for India, P6–P10 Indian), each in one approved colourway; see `P_INFO` in `build_pack.py`.
- `pack/hon-cw-<colourway>-{front,back}.svg`: one print pouch per approved logo colourway (12). Same grid and numbers, painted in that colourway.
- `logo/v2/`: logo v2, the founder's artwork (flared H and N, green O on its base), traced to vectors (`hon-logo-v2.json`) with the source JPG kept beside it. Used on the AG1-style pouches.
- `logo/wordmark/`: the Bagel Fat One wordmark. "HON" in Bagel Fat One, outlined, in 12 colourways plus letters-only files. It's generated by `logo/build_wordmark.py` (`uv run --with fonttools python3 build_wordmark.py`).
- `logo/hon-bubble-*.svg` and `logo/build_bubble.py`: the earlier LEC-style bubble badge, kept for reference.
- `ds/`: the design system source (`build_ds.py`, fonts, generated `project/`), published as the HON Design System artifact.
- `kit/kit.src.html` → `kit/index.html`: brand kit page (compliance checks, Be Bodywise teardown, logo and pouch-direction prompts, Firefly image and video prompts). It is generated, so edit the `.src` file.

Before print: replace the bracketed fields, the nutrition values (with the COA), the QR placeholder, the FSSAI logo placeholder and the EAN placeholder. Swap D6's vector rooftops for the final illustration. Clear the open items in the kit's "Fix before print" table.
