# Eatroz originality and compliance guardrails

Date: 2026-09-18

This file is a working design-control document, not legal advice. Final packaging,
claims, labels, name clearance, and launch creative require review by qualified
Indian trademark, food-regulatory, and packaging counsel.

## What may be studied

- Publicly observable information architecture, interaction patterns, responsive
  behaviour, accessibility behaviour, motion pacing, and commerce conventions.
- Broad category conventions such as a comparison table, sticky purchase action,
  product rotation, ingredient explainer, or disclosure drawer.
- Public product facts only when the fact is dated, sourced, and presented as the
  competitor's claim rather than an independently proven fact.

## What must not be copied

- Source code, compiled assets, photographs, illustrations, videos, copy, icons,
  logos, type treatments, proprietary names, or datasets.
- A competitor's distinctive combination of colour, package silhouette, label
  hierarchy, layout, imagery, or interaction choreography.
- Testimonials, clinical results, review counts, certifications, or formulation
  values belonging to another product.
- Any presentation likely to suggest affiliation, endorsement, or common origin.

## Originality controls for Eatroz

1. Implement every component locally from a blank structure.
2. Use an original package silhouette and original material/colour system.
3. Use original Eatroz copy and a different information sequence.
4. Use original generated or commissioned photography only.
5. Keep comparison columns generic unless a named comparison is reviewed and
   supported by a dated primary source.
6. Use a semantic table because it is an accessibility convention, while making
   its visual language, row taxonomy, animation, and copy distinct.
7. Keep research captures and extracted references out of production routes and
   public assets.
8. Run a pre-launch visual similarity review at desktop and mobile sizes.

## Claims and product-data controls

- Unapproved formula amounts, forms, serving size, flavour, count, price,
  certifications, testing results, FSSAI number, manufacturer, and medical claims
  remain `verification_pending` in code.
- The website is advertising under FSSAI's claims framework; customer-facing
  nutrient and health claims need substantiation and regulatory review.
- Never claim treatment, prevention, or cure of disease.
- Display competitor data as `Varies` or `Not disclosed` unless a dated source and
  like-for-like serving basis are stored.
- The visual gummy form is a concept until the contract manufacturer confirms
  shape, colour, coating, stability, and packaging compatibility.

## Pre-launch clearance checklist

- [ ] EATROZ word mark and logo search completed in relevant classes.
- [ ] Packaging silhouette and label reviewed for confusing similarity.
- [ ] Final FSSAI category and product name confirmed.
- [ ] Formula, serving basis, elemental magnesium, and percentage RDA approved.
- [ ] Ingredient, allergen, sweetener, warning, storage, and usage copy approved.
- [ ] Net quantity, MRP, batch, dates, marketer/manufacturer, customer care,
      licence, veg mark, and country-of-origin fields completed where applicable.
- [ ] Every website claim mapped to an approved label or substantiation file.
- [ ] Final product photography replaces all concept renders before live sale.
- [ ] Accessibility, responsive layout, reduced motion, and commerce mode tested.
