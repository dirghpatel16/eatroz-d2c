05 — product

Built for India from the ground up — not a Grüns clone with rupee pricing.

  • 3–5 gummies/day, not 8 — matches Indian portion norms and the cost structure the price ceiling actually supports
  • Zero / low added sugar by default — category table stakes given 237M diabetic-adjacent Indians
  • 60°C heat-stable pectin base — the single unclaimed claim in this category, anywhere, Indian or global
  • NABL lab report in every pack from day one — pre-empts the "candy dressed as vitamins" critique that hit Grüns itself

Why heat-stability is the headline, not a footnote

Gelatin gummies denature at 27–35°C. Indian summers, non-cold-chain logistics, and quick-commerce delivery bags all exceed that routinely. GCC trade press independently documents this exact failure mode at 43°C+ Gulf temperatures. Nobody else in this category has solved it.

09 — the financial model

Self-funded capital realistically produces ₹5–20 Cr by year 3. A real proof point — not acquisition scale.

₹2 Cr~₹5.5 CrSurvives, grows slowly

₹65 Cr (funded, for comparison)~₹280 CrMatches Plix/Mosaic-style trajectories

The ₹1.5–20 Cr range at ₹5 Cr capital is the single most important number in this deck. The spread comes entirely from one assumption: US-style subscription retention (~3.5 orders/yr) vs. OZiva's own founder-disclosed Indian benchmark ("even scaled-up brands see a maximum of two boxes a year"). Retention — not CAC, not price — decides which business we're actually running. That's why Phase 1's retention gate exists.

10 — risks, ranked, unprompted

Naming our own risks first is worth more than any growth number on this slide.

Price ceiling. Need 6–10x Grüns' customer count for equivalent revenue — the price gap doesn't close any other way.

Retention is the whole game. See Slide 09 — everything else is secondary until this number is proven.

Claims discipline. Nutraceuticals were 52% of FY26 F&B ad violations flagged by ASCI. No disease-adjacent language, ever.

Fast followers. HUL, Marico, USV, Dabur can all launch a competing pectin gummy quickly — heat-stability and real clinical evidence are the defensible moats, not the format itself.

Quick-commerce dependence. 30–45% all-in take; entry alone costs ₹3.75–6L before the first sale. Trial channel, not the profit engine.
