01 — problem

Every magnesium gummy in India hides the number that matters.

  • Compound weight on the label, not elemental content"400mg magnesium" — almost never what it sounds like. A second Man Matters SKU shows the exact same ambiguity.
  • A shared, unexplained bead technology"MagnaSorb" — used by two Mosaic Wellness sub-brands (Man Matters, Be Bodywise), likely in-house branding, not independently verified tech
  • Nobody has been lab-tested before getting caughtMan Matters was — and had already reformulated by the time results came out. A US magnesium gummy was FDA-recalled in 2026 for undeclared melatonin.
  • No gummy — any brand, India or US — has ever been independently lab-testedChecked directly against both active Indian testing platforms, 14 Sep 2026

India's top income decile is 74% of packaged-goods spend, 85% of beauty/personal-care spend — "masses does not mean business" (Kishore Biyani, Future Group). ₹899–999 only works if we're honest that this is who's buying: premium-reachable, D2C-literate, already a Man Matters/Be Bodywise customer.

The bet, stated honestly: sleepmaxxing is a peaking trend, not an established habit yet. Protein was a fad too, until brands invested behind it long enough to make it one (Marico's own CEO). That's the wager — not that the trend is permanent, but that we make it durable.

06 — innovation & entry barrier

The barrier isn't the formula. It's getting lab-tested before anyone makes us.

  • A formula is copyable. A trust record isn'tNaming elemental content and testing first is slow and boring to imitate credibly — that's the actual moat
  • The real objection: "less potent than tablets"A 112-upvote Reddit review of Man Matters' gummies, verbatim — naming elemental content is the direct answer
  • This isn't hypotheticalA US magnesium gummy was FDA-recalled in 2026 for undeclared melatonin. Formulation integrity is a real, current risk in this category.
The move: commission our own third-party test before launch. Publish it. Checked directly — no magnesium gummy, any brand, has ever been lab-tested by India's two active testing platforms. We'd be first, not just careful.
A cheap trust mechanic, proven twice already: mCaffeine put its CEO's phone number on every box; Minimalist's founders personally answered 100% of queries for two months. Both real, both low-cost, both transferable here.
