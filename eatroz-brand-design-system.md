## 1. Positioning

**One line:** *Sleepmaxxing, done properly.*

Magnesium-for-sleep is a real, currently-peaking trend (Gen Z leads "sleepmaxxing" adoption; the magnesium + tart-cherry "sleepy girl mocktail" ritual has held since 2023 and is still live in 2026 — verified via web search this session, not in the existing corpus). `strategy.md` Part M independently confirms the same read from the India side: unlike the original Grüns-style greens-gummy brief, magnesium's effective dose (200–400mg/day) fits inside real gummy-format constraints, and it has two live, beatable Indian competitors (Man Matters, Be Bodywise) rather than zero precedent.

Eatroz's differentiator, grounded in this project's own label-transparency finding (`HANDOFF.md` §0, Part N): name the exact chemical form on-pack (glycinate/citrate/malate/taurate) and explicitly call out **"0% Magnesium Oxide"** — the cheap, poorly-absorbed form most mass-market gummies quietly use. This is the same transparency convention Man Matters was caught reformulating around once (per Part N's Trustified/Reddit finding) — Eatroz should lead with it rather than be caught on it.

**Do not use "MagnaSorb" or any similar coined name for bead/delivery tech** — `strategy.md` Part O1 found both Man Matters and Be Bodywise already use that identical trademark, almost certainly a shared third-party-licensed ingredient tech, not either brand's own IP. Ask Softpills directly whether they have access to the same or a comparable encapsulation technology before any delivery-mechanism claim goes on-pack; if none is confirmed, don't claim one.

**Do not claim manufacturing/facility specifics** (heat tolerance, certifications beyond WHO-GMP, "ships to N countries") — `HANDOFF.md` §4 already flags these as unverified against Sanode's own public site, and this is a separate D2C sub-brand with its own manufacturing partner (Softpills), not Sanode's direct export line. Leave all such claims out until confirmed.

## 2. Color Palette

Sage + terracotta — the direct execution of `strategy.md` Part O Concept 6 ("Botanical Clinical," itself reused from Part K2's "Apothecary Sage"), matched to the founders' actual reference pack.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Primary ground | `#E7ECE3` (sage) | Pack background, primary surface |
| Accent | `#C1552F` (terracotta) | Spine stripe, logo tile, lifestyle-photo backdrops — appears once per composition as a strong block, never scattered |
| Canvas | `#F7F5F0` (warm off-white) | Negative space, secondary surface |
| Ink | `#1C1B18` (near-black) | All type, line-art motif |
| Supporting neutral | `#D8D2C4` (stone) | Dividers, tables — not a second hero color |

**Confirmed clear of every real competitor captured in `packaging-references/magnesium-gummy-design-refs/`:** Man Matters (white + periwinkle-blue), Be Bodywise (white + magenta), Naturyz (coral/salmon — the "cheap" register Part N1's Reddit research found consumers distrust, avoid), Little Joys (violet/cream, mom-and-child register, not this audience). Sage/terracotta occupies open territory.

## 3. Typography

Serif = the ritual, sans = the clinical proof — same logic Part O independently landed on for Concepts 1 and 7.

- **Display serif:** **Fraunces** (soft optical size) — confirmed via Exa search this session as ~80% visually similar to Canela, the type family the reference pack's "Magnesium Gummies" headline resembles, and it's free/Google Fonts.
- **Body/label sans:** **Inter** or **General Sans**, tight tracking, tabular figures for the nutrition table.
- Never a third typeface. Numerals in dosage/RDA callouts stay tabular.

---

## 4. Motif System

Fine line-art cross-section illustration (wood grain / mineral / root), bleeding off one edge of the pack, ~1px line weight, reads as a diagram not decoration. One motif variant per future SKU. Distinct from every competitor's approach — none of Man Matters/Be Bodywise/Naturyz/Little Joys use an illustrated diagrammatic motif; all use either flat color, a photographic ingredient burst, or a mascot.

---

## 5. Proof / Label Conventions (non-negotiable, per this project's own competitive research)

- State the exact chemical forms present (glycinate, citrate, malate, taurate) — never just "Magnesium."
- Explicit **"0% Magnesium Oxide"** callout on front or immediate sub-headline — the specific transparency marker this project's research (Part N) found builds trust against a competitor already caught reformulating.
- Exact %RDA per gummy stated on-pack, not buried on the back panel (matches the N3 finding on Man Matters' own disclosure standard).
- No delivery-tech brand name until Softpills confirms what's actually licensable (see §1).
- No specific manufacturing/facility claims until independently verified (see §1, `HANDOFF.md` §4).

---

## 6. Photography Style

Warm directional light, terracotta or stone backdrop, one hero prop only (dried branch, ceramic dish, water glass, linen), hand-interaction shots alongside still pack shots. Shot-in-a-home feel, never a white-seamless studio set or a clinical set — this is the deliberate midpoint between Man Matters' stone-slab editorial shot and Be Bodywise's flatter e-commerce studio shot (per Part O1's O1 table), pushed warmer/more ritual than either.

---

## 7. Logo Lockup Rules

- Primary: "EATROZ" wordmark, grotesque sans, wide tracking, with a standalone terracotta-tile "E" mark (favicon/app-icon usable).
- Secondary: wordmark + integrated cross-section motif as an emblem, for use without a product name nearby (deck title slides, social profile).
- Never stretch, rotate, or recolor. Terracotta tile only on sage or off-white grounds.

---

## 8. Voice

Confident, precise, warm. State the ingredient form and the %RDA. Never hype language, never "clean girl" slang, never a claim this project's own research hasn't verified.
