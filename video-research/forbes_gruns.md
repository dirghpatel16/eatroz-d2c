### **1. Key Facts & Financial Highlights**
* **Acquisition:** Unilever acquired **Grüns** for an estimated **$1.2 Billion** (announced early April).
* **Implied Multiple:** Forbes estimates Unilever paid an implied multiple of **4x trailing revenue**, making it one of the highest deal multiples in the consumer packaged goods (CPG) sector in the past year.
* **Founder Stake & Net Worth:**
  * Chad Janis retained roughly **50% ownership** after raising an estimated **$45 million** from outside investors.
  * His post-tax stake is estimated at **at least $450 million**.
* **Financial Performance & Milestones:**
  * **August 2023:** Product officially launched.
  * **2024:** Reached **$50 million** in revenue by the time Janis completed his MBA.
  * **March 2025:** Raised a **$35 million Series B** round at a **$500 million valuation**.
  * **October 2025:** Achieved an annualized run-rate of **over $300 million**.
  * **Scale:** Ships roughly **10 million gummies per day** with over **1 million customers**; ranked as the top-selling green supplement on Amazon.
* **Unilever Context:**
  * Unilever reported nearly **$60 billion** in 2025 revenue (brands include Dove, Nutrafol, and Liquid I.V.).
  * In March, Unilever merged its entire food division (including Hellmann’s and Knorr) with McCormick in a deal valued at **$45 billion** to double down on wellness and personal care.

### **4. Founder Background & Product Origin Story**
* **Early Career & Private Equity:**
  * Raised in Dallas; holds a degree in finance.
  * Former investor at **Summit Partners** (a $44 billion AUM private equity firm in Boston).
  * At Summit, he led 10 deals totaling **$1.4 billion** and served on boards/observer roles for companies like *Solo Stove*, *Brooklinen*, and *Dr. Squatch* (which Unilever acquired for $1.5B in 2025).
  * Evaluated/spoke with over 300 businesses during his investing career.
* **Stanford & Ideation:**
  * Left Summit in 2022 to attend Stanford Graduate School of Business.
  * Came up with the idea after trying a daily greens powder, disliking its chalky taste, and researching micro-nutrient gaps (noting ~90% of Americans lack adequate daily vitamin D, vitamin E, and essential minerals).
  * Spent a year developing the gummy recipe and sourcing contract manufacturers while in school.
  * Raised **~$400,000 in seed capital** directly from his Stanford MBA classmates.
* **Product Formulation:**
  * Each gummy serving contains minerals sourced from ~30 organic fruits and vegetables, 21 vitamins, and 6 grams of prebiotic fiber.
