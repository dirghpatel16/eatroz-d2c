Here is a summary of the revenue figures, distribution strategy, and unit economics shared by Nikhil Doda (co-founder of Lahori Zeera) in the video:

---

### 1. Revenue Figures & Growth Milestones

* **First Year (2017):** ~₹4 – 4.5 crores
* **Third Year:** ~₹20 crores
* **Major Benchmark:** ₹100 crores (marked the transition to pan-India scaling)
* **At last funding round (Motilal Oswal):** ~₹535 crores ARR 
* **Current Target (Current Fiscal Year):** Targeting **₹1,200 – 1,300 crores** in net revenue (~₹2,500+ crores in gross brand sales).
* **5-Year Vision:** Aiming to reach ₹3,000 – 5,000 crores in revenue.

---

### 2. Go-To-Market (GTM) & Distribution Strategy

* **General Trade (GT) Dominance:** ~95% of sales come from General Trade (Kirana stores, paan shops, local vendors across Tier-1, 2, 3 cities and rural areas). Alternate channels (Quick Commerce, Modern Trade) make up the remaining 5%.
* **Bypassing Distributors Initially:** To launch in Chandigarh without upfront distributor backing, the founders personally visited 400–500 retailers, selling single cases directly to build immediate consumer pull. Once demand was proven, local distributors proactively signed on.
* **Co-Bottling Model for Scale:** 
  * Beyond their 3 owned plants, they scale using a co-bottling joint-venture (JV) model with established beverage bottlers.
  * For a standard 500 BPM (bottles per minute) line costing ~₹25 crores, the co-bottler covers ~80% of the capital expenditure while Lahori Zeera invests ~20% (~₹5–6 crores) to ensure aligned interests.
  * Facilities are located within a 200–300 km radius of key markets to optimize freight/logistics costs and delivery times (4–5 hours lead time vs. days).
* **Single SKU / Efficiency First:** Kept a laser focus on one hero SKU (160ml at ₹10 MRP) for several years to maintain 100% production efficiency and eliminate downtime from mold changes before introducing 300ml (₹20) and 2L (₹85) variants.

---

### 3. Unit Economics & Margins (for the ₹10 MRP / ₹240 per case SKU)

* **Trade Margins:** ~30% – 32% overall channel trade margin (~20–25% for the retailer, 7–8% for the distributor).
* **GST / Taxes:** Carbonated drinks face a **40% GST rate** (increased in October 2021 from 12%).
* **Net Company Realization:** ~50% of MRP (~₹5+ per ₹10 bottle).
* **Gross Margin:** **35% – 37%** on net realization value.
