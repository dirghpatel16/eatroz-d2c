## Resolved (follow-up search, same session)
This is NOT the Mosaic Wellness "Root Labs" sub-brand (Part E3.1). It is an independent US brand, rootlabs.co, founded by Mayank Kumar (~18 months old as of Sep 2025), sourcing Ayurvedic ingredients from the Indian subcontinent for US TikTok Shop customers. The specific product is "Root Labs Liposomal Magnesium + Ashwagandha Gummies with Beadlet Technology" (rootlabs.co/products/ashwa-mag-1) — note the "beadlet technology" language, echoing Man Matters/Be Bodywise's "MagnaSorb" bead mechanic. Two distinct real companies share the "Root Labs" name; do not conflate them. See strategy.md Part O6 for the full resolution.
