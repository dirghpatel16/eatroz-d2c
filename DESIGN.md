# Design System — Eatroz (Luxury D2C Pouch Edition)

> **CORE BRAND DIRECTIVE:**  
> Eatroz is a commercial, high-converting luxury D2C e-commerce wellness brand (modeled after *Lemme, Liquid I.V., Moon Juice, Olipop*).  
> Packaging is strictly **100% STAND-UP RESEALABLE MATTE POUCH — ZERO GLASS JARS, ZERO COUNTER VESSELS**.  
> The website is a conversion-focused consumer D2C storefront, NOT a bio-research lab report or pharmaceutical audit.

---

## 1. Product Context
- **Brand:** Eatroz — Luxury Magnesium Sleep & Recovery Gummies.
- **Audience:** 18–35, urban Indian professionals, health-conscious, D2C-literate, seeking deep restorative sleep without morning melatonin grogginess.
- **Format:** Matte stand-up resealable gusset pouch with airtight click-zipper (60 vegan gummies / 30-day supply).
- **Core Value Props:**
  - 250mg Elemental Magnesium across 4 bioavailable chelates (Bisglycinate, Taurate, Malate, Citrate).
  - 0% Cheap Magnesium Oxide (Zero laxative effect).
  - 0g Added Cane Sugar (Sweetened with natural Monk Fruit & Stevia; tooth-enamel safe for bedtime).
  - 100% Citrus Fruit Pectin (Melt-proof up to 38°C in Indian summer courier transit).
  - Delicious Montmorency Tart Cherry flavor (Zero medicine/chalk taste).

---

## 2. Aesthetic Direction & Brand Mood
- **Direction:** Modern Luxury D2C & Evening Ritual Warmth. Tactile, appetizing, calming, and premium.
- **Tone:** Confident, sensory, restorative, and transparent.
- **Design Principles:**
  1. **Sensory & Delicious First:** Highlight the mouthwatering Montmorency tart cherry flavor and 0g sugar benefits.
  2. **High-Converting Buy Box:** Classic 2-column split hero with pouch gallery, tiered volume bundles (1x, 2x, 3x), and Indian pincode delivery estimator.
  3. **No Pharma Lab Trap:** Never display raw chemical equations ($C_4H_8MgN_2O_4$), "formulation pending" warning banners, or clinical widgets above the fold.
  4. **Pouch Ergonomics:** Showcase the lightweight, shatterproof, travel-ready advantages of the stand-up pouch over bulky glass jars.

## 4. Typography Scale

- **Display & Headlines:** **Fraunces** (Soft optical size serif) — Provides warmth, bedtime calm, and luxury editorial presence.
- **Body & UI Elements:** **Inter** (Geometric sans) — Tight tracking, high legibility for product descriptions, price tags, and buttons.
- **Data & Tables:** **IBM Plex Mono** (Tabular numbers) — Used exclusively for dosage amounts (250mg), %RDA, and delivery dates.

---

## 5. Packaging Standards (Non-Negotiable)

1. **100% Matte Stand-Up Gusset Pouch:** Resealable airtight click-zipper, tear notches, 15g tare weight.
2. **Never Design Glass Jars:** Glass shatters in Indian couriers (Delhivery / BlueDart), adds 350g of unnecessary freight weight, and feels outdated.
3. **Airtight Hermetic Barrier:** Shuts out humidity and moisture; keeps gummies chewy and fresh for 12 months.
4. **On-Pack Declarations:**
   - 250mg Elemental Magnesium
   - 0g Added Sugar (Monk Fruit Sweetened)
   - 100% Vegan Citrus Pectin (Never Melts)
   - FSSAI License & Green Vegetarian Dot
