# Slide 2 — The insight

**Grüns didn't invent a nutrient. It invented a format — and sold to Unilever for $1.2B in 32 months.**

- 8 gummies/day replacing a chalky greens-powder shaker
- Profitable by month 14, on $45–55M raised total
- Reached $300M+ ARR before exit; 1M+ customers, 10M gummies/day

# Slide 3 — Why the literal playbook doesn't transplant

| | Grüns (US) | India reality |
|---|---|---|
| Price | ~₹150–250/day | ₹15–35/day (category ceiling) |
| Sugar | 8g added sugar/serving | 237M diabetic/prediabetic; category norm is zero-sugar |
| Format | 8 gummies/day | No Indian brand doses above 1–2/day |
| Exit precedent | $1.2B is real | No Indian nutraceutical brand has ever reached $1B exit scale |

*Speaker note: this is the credibility slide. Showing you know exactly why the obvious play fails is what earns the room's trust for the next 15 slides.*

# Slide 4 — The honest bar, stated up front

**Self-funded, India-only, gets you to $70–250M (₹150–400 Cr revenue at a 4–6x strategic exit multiple) — not the full $1B/$500M ARR ambition.**

**Reaching that bar needs two stages:**
1. India-first, capital-efficient, self-funded proof (Years 1–2)
2. A funded export/international stage — GCC first, then possibly US/UK — once retention is proven (Year 2+)

*Speaker note: say this before anyone else in the room does the math and catches you overclaiming. It is the single most credibility-building slide in the deck.*

# Slide 5 — The white space, precisely

**No Indian brand occupies: lifestyle-branded, multi-benefit, daily-ritual nutrition, in gummy form, at ₹40–70/day.**

- Supply6 (powder sachet) and Wellbeing Nutrition (effervescent) occupy this price/positioning band — just not in gummies
- Supply6 raised its round from **Unilever Ventures** in July 2026 — the same Unilever that bought Grüns
- The one Indian brand that tried gummies-as-the-category (Power Gummies) collapsed 80% and sold its assets in distress — because it had no credibility layer, not because gummies don't work

*Speaker note: Unilever Ventures backing Supply6 is your single best borrowed-credibility data point. Let it land.*

---

# Slide 6 — Product

**A multi-gummy daily ritual, built for India from the ground up — not a Grüns clone.**

- 3–5 gummies/day (not 8) — matches Indian portion norms and cost structure
- Zero/low added sugar by default — category table stakes, not a differentiator to skip
- **60°C heat-stable pectin base — the single unclaimed claim in this entire category**, Indian or global
- NABL lab report in every pack, from day one — pre-empts the "candy dressed as vitamins" critique that hit Grüns itself

*Speaker note: the heat-stability claim is real and independently evidenced (GCC trade press documents gelatin gummies failing above 27-35°C against 43°C+ Gulf summer temps) — use it as the headline technical differentiator, every time.*

*Speaker note: this slide is here to preempt the "so is this just Grüns-but-Indian" question with a real point of view.*

# Slide 10 — Go-to-market

**D2C-first, prepaid-biased, subscription from day one — not ad-spend-led.**

1. **Phase 0 (Mo 1–4):** Formulation + FSSAI Relabeller licence + NABL partner + D2C site + Amazon (0% referral fee under ₹1,000)
2. **Phase 1 (Mo 4–12):** Prove retention ≥35% at month 2–3 before scaling spend. One hero SKU. Nano/micro creator pipeline (₹8K–80K/deliverable), not celebrity-tier.
3. **Phase 2 (Year 2):** Quick commerce for trial (~₹5–10L launch cost), second SKU (effervescent, hedges format risk)
4. **Phase 3 (Mo 18–24):** Raise, once retention is proven — not year 3, whenever the metric clears

*Speaker note: month-2/3 retention ≥35% is the single load-bearing number in this entire plan. Say that explicitly.*

# Slide 13 — Risks, ranked

1. Price ceiling / revenue-per-customer gap vs. Grüns (need 6–10x the customers for equivalent revenue)
2. CAC inflation + quick-commerce dependence (30–45% all-in take)
3. FSSAI/ASCI claims discipline (52% of FY26 F&B ad violations were nutraceuticals)
4. Sugar/diabetes sensitivity (237M diabetic/prediabetic Indians)
5. Fast-follower risk — HUL, Marico, USV, Dabur can all launch a competing pectin gummy quickly
6. Retention is the whole game (Slide 12) — everything else is secondary to proving this number

*Speaker note: naming your own risks, ranked, unprompted, is worth more credibility than any single growth number in this deck.*
