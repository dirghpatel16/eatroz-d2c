# Brand Research Batch 3 — 20 Additional Brands (33 → 53 total)

Date: 15 Sep 2026. Done inline via batched WebSearch (not subagents), per explicit steer to keep usage economical. Covers: Indian D2C craft references (boAt, Mokobara, Nykaa, Dot & Key, Pilgrim), Indian supplement-specific brands (Setu Nutrition, Zeroharm, GNC India, Nutrova), US supplement brands (Garden of Life, Elysium Health, Nutrafol, Bloom Nutrition, Momentous, Thorne, LMNT, Liquid IV), plus Country Delight (adjacent D2C).

## Strong signal

### Setu Nutrition (India, direct supplement-category peer) — the most important find in this batch
- **Color-coded-by-purpose system**: each SKU gets its own color mapped to what it does, not a single brand hero color — "Deep Sleep is a drowsy dark purple, Skin Renew a refreshing apricot." Built by Delhi studio Animal, explicitly to break the category's "clinical/medicinal" feel.
- **Direct cross-validation of an earlier finding**: the India sleep-brand batch (`brand-research-batch-india-sleep.md`) independently concluded a disciplined dusk/indigo hue is an open lane nobody's claimed for sleep specifically. Setu is now a second, independent data point that **purple/dark-violet already reads as "sleep" in the Indian supplement category** (their actual live SKU color choice). This doesn't argue for abandoning sage/terracotta — but it's now a twice-confirmed, real market signal worth having on record.
- Source: [Animal — Setu](https://www.weareanimal.co/setu)

### boAt (India D2C, adjacent category — electronics/lifestyle, craft reference not competitor)
- Dark charcoal `#1a2024` + vibrant red `#e91c24`, deliberately loud/energetic — direct opposite of Eatroz's restrained approach, useful as a clear contrast case for "what loud-Indian-D2C looks like at massive scale."
- The lowercase-capital "boAt" stylization is a deliberate, simple differentiation device worth noting as a technique (not copying the specific treatment).
- Source: [logotyp.us](https://logotyp.us/logo/boat/), [Product Monk](https://www.productmonk.io/p/boat-branding-strategy)

### Mokobara (India D2C luggage, craft reference)
- One additional finding worth stealing as a technique: **a single, disciplined signature color used only as an interior accent** (their yellow lining — "the brand literally never doesn't use yellow"), while the exterior stays restrained. This is structurally close to Eatroz's terracotta-as-single-accent rule — real proof the discipline works at a premium Indian D2C brand, in a totally different category.
- Source: [Morrama](https://www.morrama.com/projects/mokobara), [The Hard Copy](https://thehardcopy.co/mokobara-brand-case-study/)

### Nykaa (India beauty retail, craft reference)
- Single hero color (`#fc2779` pink) carried across a full custom typographic wordmark, explicitly built over two years to feel like "a natural extension of the lifestyle" of its core customer — a real example of long-horizon brand discipline, not a quick launch palette.
- Source: [Thought Over Design](https://thoughtoverdesign.com/work/nykaa)

### Dot & Key (India skincare, closest adjacent-category ingredient-transparency peer)
- Explicitly combines "science with a relatable tone" — ingredient-focused and playful/vibrant rather than clinical, a different resolution of the same transparency-vs-warmth tension Eatroz is navigating. Worth noting as a counter-example: they chose vibrancy over calm to signal trust, where Eatroz has chosen calm — both are legitimate resolutions, not a signal Eatroz is wrong.
- Source: [Studio Glyph](https://www.studioglyph.in/dot-key), [Proudly Made in India](https://proudlymadeinindia.com/dot-key-indias-quirky-skincare-brand/)

### Garden of Life (US supplements, legacy/mass-natural reference point)
- Illustrated circular logo (farmer, garden scene, fruits/vegetables), blue banner, serif+cursive type mix — an older "naturalist" register most premium 2020s+ brands have moved away from. Useful negative reference: this is what "natural supplement branding" looked like a decade ago; Eatroz's diagrammatic line-art motif is a deliberate departure from this illustrated/pastoral style, not an accident.
- Source: [1000logos.net](https://1000logos.net/garden-of-life-logo/)

### Liquid IV (US hydration, adjacent category)
- 2024 rebrand case study: bolder typography, brighter palette, a logo where negative space in "IV" reveals a drip — cited results (purchase intent, shelf findability, "less medicinal" brand association) are a real data point that visual boldness can directly reduce a "medicinal" feel, worth knowing even though Eatroz is taking the calm-not-loud path instead.
- Source: [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/liquid-iv-debuts-new-brand-identity-marking-its-first-refresh-in-over-a-decade-302132411.html), [Marketing Dive](https://www.marketingdive.com/news/liquid-iv-unilever-brand-refresh-summer-campaign/715089/)

## Updated synthesis note

Combined with the prior two research files (53 brands total now), the single most load-bearing new finding is the **Setu Nutrition cross-validation of the dusk/purple "sleep" signal color** — two independent Indian sources (this batch + the earlier sleep-brand batch) now agree on the same open territory. This remains a "worth knowing" finding, not a recommendation to reopen the locked sage/terracotta palette — logged here so it's on record if the founders ever revisit a sleep-specific sub-line or SKU-level color coding within the existing system (Setu's model — one brand, color-coded by product purpose — could work as a *secondary* layer under Eatroz's primary sage/terracotta identity, the same way Setu still has one master brand with per-SKU accent colors).
