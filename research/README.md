# Eatroz — Market, Competitor & Consumer Research

This directory contains categorized research compiled during the brand formulation and strategy phases.

## Directory Structure

- **`market-strategy/`**:
  - `strategy.md`: Comprehensive strategy document covering unit economics, formulation boundaries, and D2C channel architecture.
  - `India_Gummy_Wellness_Strategy.pdf`: Comprehensive strategy report on Indian nutraceutical gummy dynamics.
- **`competitors/`**:
  - Competitive visual audits across 50+ wellness brands (Man Matters, Be Bodywise, Setu Nutrition, Wellbeing Nutrition, BiteBella, Goli, etc.).
  - Sourced consumer reviews highlighting format trust issues and packaging weaknesses in legacy brands.
- **`consumer-insights/`**:
  - `gen-z-purchase-psychology-2026.md`: Insights into the 18–35 demographic, "sleepmaxxing" rituals, and demand for 0g sugar and clean ingredients.
  - `visual-packaging-deconstruction.md`: Ergonomic breakdown of pouches vs jars in Indian courier logistics.
