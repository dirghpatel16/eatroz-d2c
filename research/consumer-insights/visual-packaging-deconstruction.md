# Visual Packaging Deconstruction: Functional Wellness and Gummy Brands

## Scope and method

This is a visual-design audit of the current commercial packaging shown on official product pages and retailer packshots, conducted 12 September 2026. A multimodal inspection of current pack images was completed for Grüns, OLLY, Lemme, Wellbeing Nutrition, Plix, What’s Up Wellness, Kapiva, OZiva and HK Vitals; remaining brands are assessed from current official product/retailer evidence. It focuses on visible form, hierarchy, palette, typography, cues of proof, and likely performance in physical retail and a quick-commerce thumbnail. It deliberately avoids treating marketing imagery as evidence of unsupported formulation claims. When a material is not explicitly stated by the brand, it is described only as a visible observation (for example, “clear cylindrical jar”), not as a technical specification.

**Reading key:**

- **Silhouette:** the recognition asset before a consumer reads the label.
- **FoP hierarchy:** the order in which the front panel is likely read.
- **Design job:** what the visual system makes the consumer feel or infer.
- **Opportunity / warning:** transferable lesson, not a judgement on product efficacy.

### 1. Grüns — daily nutrition as snack system

| Lens | Visual deconstruction |
| --- | --- |
| Silhouette | Bright green, resealable outer pouch with a matching individual sachet; small green bear-shaped gummies are commonly pictured beside it. The parent pouch and the daily dose look intentionally like one scalable object. |
| Color | The saturated “greens” field is the category disruptor. Yellow/cream type and high-contrast functional callouts keep a dark green product from becoming dour or pharmacy-like. |
| Type and hierarchy | `grüns` is large and friendly; “Superfoods Greens Gummies” / comprehensive-nutrition proposition follows; daily-pack count and benefit/proof cues come next. The word “gummy” and the product form are visible early, preventing the greens proposition from becoming abstract. |
| Graphic language | Ingredient/benefit icons, visible bears, flavor language, and culture collaborations introduce snack-world energy. The medical/proof layer is intentionally deeper in the product page, not the entire front panel. |
| Design job | Converts a large, complex nutritional proposition into a one-pack ritual. The pack itself makes portability and adherence part of the evidence. |
| Shelf and thumbnail | Very strong on a white e-commerce background because a single green block is instantly recognizable. On shelf, it behaves more like a premium snack pouch than a vitamin jar. Individual sachets become social content and sampling units. |
| Opportunity / warning | The cohesive outer + inner system is worth studying. Do not copy the all-green field unless the new brand owns a different visual reason to use it; a monocolor masterbrand can become category shorthand for “greens.” |

## Cross-brand visual findings

### The five form-factor archetypes

| Archetype | Brands | What it signals | Strategic use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Clear / squat gummy jar | OLLY, Plix, What’s Up, Power Gummies, HK Vitals | Familiarity, taste, repeat, accessible routine. | Use only if a proprietary silhouette/cap/label system makes it distinct. |
| Design-object bottle | Ritual, Lemme | Considered premium, display, beauty or transparency. | Best for high-margin targeted SKUs and retail where physical pickup matters. |
| Resealable outer pouch + daily sachets | Grüns, Create | Portability, adherence, freshness, snack-like routine. | Best when one daily dose is the actual product behavior. |
| Ritual ecosystem | AG1 | Commitment, foundational habit, subscription. | Build after a hero product has earned repeat. |
| Flat strip/carton or tube | Wellbeing Nutrition, OZiva | Format innovation, technology, sampling, pharmacy/beauty differentiation. | Strong opportunity where water-free or single-dose utility is real. |

### The four hierarchy patterns

1. **Brand → mood benefit → actives**: OLLY. Best for reducing consumer cognitive load.
2. **Brand → product system → proof**: Grüns, AG1, Supply6. Best for high-AOV daily habit platforms.
3. **Brand → aesthetic desire → targeted benefit**: Lemme. Best where beauty and culture can carry trial.
4. **Concern → active/quantity → compliance/trust**: Wellbeing Nutrition, What’s Up, HK Vitals, OZiva. Best for India’s research-oriented marketplace shopper.

### The actual white space

The new brand should not choose between “fun” and “credible.” The white space is a **calm, ownable masterbrand object with a rapid Indian proof strip**:

- Keep one clear masterbrand color/silhouette fixed across the portfolio.
- Put a plain-language benefit in the upper half of the label.
- Give one hero active or dose a single dedicated proof zone; no ingredient wallpaper.
- Use a consistent QR-tested / batch-transparency convention on every SKU.
- Make the source pack satisfy both a 140–200 px mobile tile and a three-foot shelf block.

The strongest platform for this brief remains **Modern Apothecary / Clinical Chic (Option A)**, enhanced by **Functional Confectionery’s sensory content language (Option B)**. A softer, colorful fruit image can sit in ecommerce/social assets while the physical pack keeps its distinctive proof-led architecture.

## Design brief to take into prototyping

Create three competing front panels for the same hero SKU before naming more products:

1. **A-01, clinical masterbrand:** opaque soft-square jar, chalk/ink/citron, benefit `GUT DAILY`, one exact proof line.
2. **A-02, clinical + appetite:** same jar and hierarchy, but adds one controlled fruit/gummy macro field for quicker emotional trial.
3. **C-01, dose-system:** 30-day dispenser with numbered daily packs; no jar unless testing proves the jar wins repeat/price acceptance.

Test every direction in three states: actual-size held in hand, a Target/modern-trade shelf composite, and a 200 px Blinkit/Zepto-style grid next to OLLY-like and Indian bright-jar competitors. The design that wins only at full scale should not go to production.
