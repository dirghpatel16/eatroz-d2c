# Eatroz — Operational Guidelines

## Brand & Product Core

Eatroz is a luxury consumer D2C e-commerce wellness brand in India, specializing in bioavailable magnesium sleep and recovery gummies.

- **Packaging Format:** 100% Matte Stand-Up Resealable Pouch with Airtight Click-Zipper (60 vegan gummies / 30-day supply).
- **Core Formulation:** 250mg Elemental Magnesium across 4 Bioavailable Chelates (Bisglycinate, Taurate, Malate, Citrate). 0% Magnesium Oxide.
- **Flavor & Integrity:** European Montmorency Tart Cherry · 0g Added Cane Sugar (Monk fruit & Stevia) · 100% Citrus Fruit Pectin (Heat-stable up to 38°C).
- **Bundle Pricing:**
  - 1 Pouch (30 Days): **₹899** (Starter)
  - 2 Pouches (60 Days): **₹1,599** (⭐ Most Popular — Free Express Delivery)
  - 3 Pouches (90 Days): **₹2,199** (🏆 Best Value — Free Silk Sleep Mask + Express Delivery)

## Storefront Architecture & Standards

- **Single Source of Truth:** Always refer to **`D2C-POUCH-STOREFRONT-SPEC.md`** for all storefront layout, wireframe, and copy requirements.
- **Design System:** Follow **`DESIGN.md`** for palette (Sage `#E7ECE3`, Canvas `#F7F5F0`, Terracotta `#C1552F`, Deep Ink `#1C1B18`) and typography (Fraunces serif for display, Inter sans for UI/body).
- **Interactive Motion:** Use browser-native CSS, HTML5 Canvas, SVG, or GSAP for all web animations and interactions.
