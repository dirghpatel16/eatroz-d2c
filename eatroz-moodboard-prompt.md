# Eatroz Moodboard — Image Agent Prompt

Paste directly into your image agent (same tool used for the two founder reference images). Produces one labeled grid image — style direction only, not final packaging. Grounded in `eatroz-brand-design-system.md`; do not use claim/tech language flagged there (no "MagnaSorb," no manufacturing/facility specifics).

---

## Prompt

```
Create a single moodboard image: a clean 6-cell labeled grid on a neutral warm off-white background (#F7F5F0), even margins, soft drop shadow under each cell, small italic serif captions under each cell reading "Concept 1" through "Concept 6". Overall theme: "Botanical Clinical" — a magnesium sleep-gummy wellness brand called Eatroz, for 18-35 year old Indian consumers who are ingredient-literate and into the "sleepmaxxing" evening-ritual trend. Balance clinical precision with warm, lived-in ritual — never a sterile lab, never a candy-colored kids' supplement, and never the bright white-plus-color-block look of Indian competitor magnesium gummy brands (avoid periwinkle-blue-on-white and magenta-on-white specifically).

Strict palette across every cell: sage green #E7ECE3 (primary surface), terracotta/rust #C1552F (single accent — appears once per cell as a strong block: fabric, backdrop wash, or object), warm off-white #F7F5F0 (negative space), near-black #1C1B18 (ink/shadow), muted stone #D8D2C4 (secondary neutral). No other colors.

Typography reference for any pack mockups in-frame: display serif headline in Fraunces (soft optical size) — warm, high-contrast, editorial, not a generic system serif. Logotype/labels in a tight grotesque sans (Inter or General Sans), wide letter-spacing, all-caps.

Cell 1 — "Evening ritual": a hand mid-motion placing a single gummy into a small ceramic dish on a terracotta-washed surface, soft low-angle warm light, one dried botanical sprig in frame, nothing else.

Cell 2 — "Ingredient honesty": a macro flatlay of a few gummies next to raw mineral-adjacent visual texture (a crystal/mineral fragment, not literal packaging), shot on stone, hard directional shadow, editorial not decorative.

Cell 3 — "Pack presence": a stand-up pouch mockup (sage body, terracotta spine stripe, fine line-art cross-section illustration bleeding off one edge, serif product name "EATROZ", small grotesque-sans wordmark tile) sitting on a plain sage-toned surface, product photography lighting, centered.

Cell 4 — "Night table styling": a lifestyle scene — glass of water, the pouch, a folded linen cloth, on a nightstand near a window at dusk, terracotta-toned ambient light, shallow depth of field.

Cell 5 — "Line-art motif study": a close, cropped view of the fine botanical/mineral cross-section illustration alone (no product), pure line art on warm off-white, ink-black lines only, as if a diagram from a nature journal.

Cell 6 — "Palette + type swatch": a clean flat-lay swatch cell showing the 5 palette colors as small squares with hex labels beneath in the grotesque sans, and the words "Magnesium Gummies" set once in the Fraunces serif and once in the grotesque sans for comparison.

No text anywhere except the specified captions, hex labels, and the two typography samples in cell 6. No competitor logos, no stock-photo watermarks, no generic wellness clichés (no yoga poses, no green smoothies, no lotus flowers). Photography throughout should look shot in a real home, styled but lived-in, never a white-seamless studio set.
```
