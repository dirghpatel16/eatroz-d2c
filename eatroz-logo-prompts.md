# Eatroz Logo — Image Agent Prompt Specs

Four wordmark directions, all built from `eatroz-brand-design-system.md`'s locked palette (sage `#E7ECE3` / terracotta `#C1552F` / ink `#1C1B18` / off-white `#F7F5F0`) and type pairing (Fraunces display serif, Inter/General Sans grotesque). Paste each block into your image agent individually — logo generation works better one direction at a time than batched.

---

## Direction 1 — Pure wordmark + tile mark (closest to the founders' existing pack)

```
Generate a logo design sheet for a wordmark "EATROZ", on a clean off-white background (#F7F5F0). Primary lockup: the word EATROZ set in a tight, confident grotesque sans typeface (style of Inter or General Sans), medium-wide letter tracking, all-caps, near-black (#1C1B18). Directly beside or above it, a small solid square tile in terracotta (#C1552F) containing a single capital "E" in off-white, matching the wordmark's letterforms — usable standalone as an app icon or favicon. Show: (1) the full horizontal lockup, (2) the tile mark alone at three sizes to test legibility down to 32px, (3) the wordmark alone without the tile. No taglines, no additional graphic elements, no gradients, no drop shadows. Flat vector-style design, precise geometry, plenty of white space around each version.
```

---

## Direction 2 — Wordmark + integrated botanical emblem

```
Generate a logo design sheet for the brand "EATROZ". Primary lockup: the wordmark EATROZ in a tight grotesque sans (Inter/General Sans style), near-black (#1C1B18), paired with a small circular or shield-shaped emblem to its left containing a fine single-line-weight illustration of a mineral/wood-grain cross-section (concentric organic rings, like a tree ring or geode slice) drawn only in terracotta (#C1552F) line art on a sage (#E7ECE3) circular ground. Show the emblem large and isolated, then the full lockup, then the emblem alone as a standalone mark for use without the wordmark nearby (social profile picture use case). Flat vector, thin consistent line weight (~1-2px equivalent), no gradients, no photographic texture, clean background #F7F5F0.
```

---

## Direction 3 — Lettermark / monogram badge

```
Generate a logo design sheet exploring a monogram mark for the brand "EATROZ". Design a circular or softly-rounded-square badge containing a custom-drawn "E" letterform in a warm serif style (in the spirit of Fraunces — high contrast strokes, slightly editorial), off-white (#F7F5F0) letterform on a solid terracotta (#C1552F) badge ground, with a thin sage-green (#E7ECE3) ring outline. Show the badge alone at three sizes, then paired with the full EATROZ wordmark set below it in a tight grotesque sans, near-black. This direction should feel like a premium apothecary stamp, not a corporate crest — no laurels, no ornate flourishes, no Latin mottos. Flat vector, clean background.
```

---

## Direction 4 — Wordmark with integrated motif inside a letterform

```
Generate a logo design sheet for the wordmark "EATROZ" where the letter "O" is replaced with a custom circular device: a fine terracotta (#C1552F) line-art illustration of a mineral or wood cross-section, sized and weighted to sit naturally inside the wordmark's letterform proportions, rest of the wordmark in a tight grotesque sans (Inter/General Sans style), near-black (#1C1B18). Show the full wordmark lockup at large size, a close crop on just the modified "O" to test it reads clearly as both a letterform and a motif, and a version of the "O" device alone as a standalone mark. Flat vector, no gradients, no drop shadows, background #F7F5F0.
```

---

## Selection notes
- Direction 1 is the safest, closest to what's already validated in the founder reference pack.
- Directions 2 and 4 reuse the cross-section motif from the moodboard/design system — pick whichever tests better at small size (32-64px) before committing, since `visual-packaging-deconstruction.md`'s own India findings stress thumbnail legibility (Blinkit/Zepto 140px rule) as a hard constraint, not an aesthetic nice-to-have.
- Direction 3 is the highest-differentiation option against every competitor in `packaging-references/magnesium-gummy-design-refs/` — none use a badge/monogram convention.
- Test all four against the same 140px thumbnail rule from `brand-architecture-report.md` §2 before finalizing.
