* **Climate Resilience:** 100% Citrus Fruit Pectin (Gelatin-free, heat-stable up to 38°C — guaranteed never to melt into a sticky brick during Indian summer courier transit).
* **Active Chelates:** 250mg Bioavailable Elemental Magnesium (Bisglycinate, Taurate, Malate, Citrate) — 0mg synthetic melatonin, non-habit forming.

---

## 2. Why the Pouch Concept Wins in Indian D2C (Strategic Rationale)

1. **Zero Breakage in Indian Logistics:** Glass jars frequently shatter in Delhivery / BlueDart transit. Stand-up pouches are indestructible and lightweight.
2. **Reduced Shipping & Carbon Costs:** Pouch tare weight is ~15g vs. ~350g for amber glass jars, dramatically lowering logistics overhead and enabling free shipping on 2+ pouch bundles.
3. **Multi-Pouch Volume Stacking:** Selling 2-pack and 3-pack pouch bundles is intuitive, tactile, and visually satisfying for consumers (e.g. Lemme, Liquid I.V., Moon Juice).
4. **Bedside & Travel Friendly:** Consumers can toss an airtight pouch into their gym bag, backpack, or carry-on without bulky pill bottles rattling.
